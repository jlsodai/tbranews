Programme overview

 

The first cohort of 12 students has begun a three-month course at the Menzies Aviation Training School in Uganda. Participants are working toward internationally recognised IATA qualifications in one of two tracks:

Airline Customer
Service Diploma

Cargo Introductory Diploma


Graduates will move into internships in operational areas at Entebbe International Airport. Employment will follow where roles are available, subject to standard recruitment requirements, qualifications and performance.

The Business Case for Inclusive Workplaces

 



Inclusion is a performance driver as well as a values commitment. McKinsey research covering more than 1,000 companies found that those in the top quartile for ethnic diversity on executive teams were 36% more likely to outperform financially than those in the bottom quartile. The same research links diversity to better access to talent, stronger decision-making and higher employee engagement.

For employers, this means inclusion goes beyond representation. It widens the talent pool, surfaces skills that might otherwise be overlooked, and builds workplaces where people from different backgrounds can contribute fully. Refugee employment is one way to put this into practice, creating social and economic value while strengthening workforce resilience.
 

Building on a Proven Model

The Uganda programme extends Menzies Aviation's broader commitment to refugee employment. The company has already enrolled 120 refugees into work across its non-African markets. Bringing this experience to Africa replicates a model built to create sustainable pathways into aviation careers.

Designed to Scale

 

The Uganda programme is not a one-off initiative. Menzies Aviation and Amahoro Coalition will evaluate the first cohort, apply the lessons learned and expand the model to other African markets, including Kenya and Côte d'Ivoire.

Partner with Amahoro Coalition

Market Access:

Venture Funding:

We invest directly in displaced-led businesses and connect high-potential ventures to development finance to fuel growth, jobs and economic opportunity.
