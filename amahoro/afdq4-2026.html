In just 25 days, Amahoro Coalition and our co-conveners (INKOMOKO and UNHCR) will bring together leaders from business, government, philanthropy and communities affected by displacement will gather in Addis Ababa to ask a vital question: How can we build an African economy where everyone has the opportunity to contribute?

From 16–18 November 2026, the fourth Africa Private Sector Forum on Displacement will bring together people with lived experience of displacement, entrepreneurs, investors, policymakers and advocates. Together, they will explore how to turn economic inclusion from an ambition into action.

The 3-day programme at a glance

Monday 16 November

Welcome & Historical context

Delegates will visit the Adwa Victory Museum before gathering at Africa Hall for the Forum opening. The event will explore how Africa’s history of displacement, exile and liberation connects to the challenge of building a more inclusive economic future. A networking reception will follow.

Tuesday 17 November

Ideas into action

At the Adwa Victory Museum, delegates can explore the Corridor of Evidence, visit the Marketplace of businesses from displaced and host communities and discover the museum. The programme will reflect on the journey and commitments of previous Forums, then explore six areas of opportunity: agriculture, finance, manufacturing, consumer goods and supply chains, investment, and the creative economy. Sessions will also spotlight Ethiopia, examine policy changes that can support inclusive economies, consider how philanthropic capital can help solutions grow, and hear from young leaders. The day will conclude with verified commitments and an Africa 2063 Time Capsule, followed by an awards dinner celebrating Ethiopian inclusion leaders and unveiling a new Africa’s Most Influential FDPs publication.


In the evening, celebrate the people and ideas moving this work forward at the Solutions Dinner & Awards, with recognition and live music.

Wednesday 18 November

Building what’s next

The Forum’s final day will feature co-convener-led technical conversations on building a more inclusive economic ecosystem across the continent. By invitation, the sessions will bring together practitioners, funders, policymakers and people with lived experience.

AFD 2026 Venues

The Forum will take place across two landmark venues in Addis Ababa.

Adwa Victory Memorial Museum commemorates the 1896 Battle of Adwa, when Ethiopian forces defeated the Italian invading army and preserved the country’s independence. The victory became a powerful symbol of African resistance to colonialism. The Museum will host the Marketplace, activations and plenary sessions.

Discover the Museum →


Africa Hall, at the United Nations Economic Commission for Africa, is where the Organization of African Unity Charter was adopted and signed in 1963. Its striking foyer features Total Liberation of Africa, a stained-glass work by Ethiopian artist Maître Afework Tekle.
