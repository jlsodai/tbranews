Integrate Health makes government health systems work for patients, filling the implementation gap between communities and ministries across Francophone West Africa, a nine-country bloc of some 175 million people that receives less development investment than its Anglophone neighbours and carries among the highest maternal and child mortality rates in the world. Founded in 2004, the organisation now enters its third decade with a budget moving from USD 12.5 million to a projected USD 14 million, and roughly 35 active funding partners.

For over 20 years, Integrate Health has partnered with governments and local communities to integrate professional, salaried Community Health Workers into strengthened public health systems. Integrate Health currently operates in the US, Togo and Guinea, with Senegal next on the horizon, the next chapter of a deliberate five-year strategy. The organisation is proudly locally rooted: roughly 95% of staff identify as African, and around 70% of the leadership team is African and based in Africa. It's earned the confidence of some of the most rigorous funders in global health, including Rippleworks, Jasmine Social Investments, and Mulago Foundation.

Integrate Health is seeking a Senior Director of Human Resources to lead this function through its next chapter of growth.

This role extends well beyond traditional human resources leadership. During the first six months, the Senior Director will assess the eight-person HR team supporting Togo and Guinea, alongside the Global Support Team, while determining the people structure and capabilities needed to support Integrate Health’s expansion into Senegal. The Senior Director will make the decisions required to align the function with the organisation’s evolving strategy and serve as the Executive Team’s principal thought partner on all people-related matters.

The ideal candidate will bring significant senior-level human resources leadership experience and be fluent in both French and English, alongside other key capabilities.
